# Bounded adjacent-layer compatibility probes

20 October 2026, Asia/Taipei. The stable full-trajectory ablations completed normally. Full/no-TV/no-HF/no-perceptual scores on the reused 24-clip development pilot were respectively **74.7368/76.0000/74.7368/78.7368% WER** and **3.3186/3.3226/3.3058/3.3504 UTMOS**. Full objective has the highest DNSMOS; removing HF worsens all quality means. Removing both perceptual terms adds only 0.0318 UTMOS while the full control is 0.2714 below the published target. These results do not reproduce the paper's joint WER/quality loss-guidance pattern here, and do not justify another loss-weight grid or promoting an ablation as the full objective.

The paper specifies WavLM-Large and HiFi-GAN without identifying the encoder output layer or the vocoder's training artifacts. Our available generator is explicitly trained for WavLM layer 6. **Keep the production loader's matching-layer guard intact.** This isolated study deliberately probes the two adjacent encoder outputs, layers **5 and 7**, as inputs to that unchanged layer-6-trained generator. Neither is assumed to be a matching upstream or author contract. Do not extend the layer search based on these outcomes.

Freeze the same 24 IDs/audio hashes, original transcripts, raw-input WavLM weights, regular HiFi-GAN, scorer weights/runtime and all previous sources/results. Load the original layer-6 pair through the unchanged production loader; create separate experimental encoder wrappers sharing the frozen WavLM weights. Use no kNN conversion, training, feature normalization, waveform gain adjustment, duration warping or sample filtering.

First extract new layer-5/7 q and reconstruction WAVs for all 24 clips under the validated strict-math CUDA policy, cuBLAS workspace `:4096:8`, TF32/benchmarking disabled. Compare fresh layer-6 output with the frozen layer-6 q on every clip (relative L2 <=1e-5 and max absolute <=1e-4; never relax after seeing results). Repeat each experimental extraction on the first fixed clip and require exact equality. Record q norm ratios/cosines/differences against layer 6. These are diagnostics, not criteria for selecting clips.

Score both reconstruction sets with the unchanged native ASR and fixed DNSMOS OVRL, NISQA overall and UTMOS22 strong. Verify all waveform hashes/counts/aggregation, require original-clean scores/protocol to match the frozen control, independently re-extract all 24 q bitwise and resynthesize every reconstruction. A layer passes only when reconstruction WER is at most **2 points above the layer-6 control**, and **each** reconstruction MOS mean is at least the layer-6 value minus **0.1**, with all MOS>2.5. Publish both passing and failing results. A scientific gate failure skips that layer's attacks; an unexpected error fails the job.

Only passing layers receive the existing full-objective 50-step collector at constant Adam LR=0.1, zero initialization, rho=0.1, lambda=1, alpha=1, fc=6000, sum lexical NLL/original-case/no-timestamps and original-clean TV/HF denominators. No production attack or loss definition changes. The feature radius is relative to the current layer's q, as in the equation; report its norm relative to baseline so this representation change is inspectable. Save every projected iterate and final audio/optimizer evidence. Decode and score the saved FLOAT WAVs using the unchanged evaluation path. Re-extract q and resynthesize returned deltas during verification.

A reconstruction pass is empirical screening and does not make layer-6-trained weights an identified matching layer-5/7 author vocoder. WER/MOS comparisons remain development evidence.
