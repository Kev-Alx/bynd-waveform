# Source audit: quality and computational cost

Prepared 26 October 2026, Asia/Taipei. This is a narrowly scoped source/code audit supporting the next experiment, not a new attack result. It reuses the completed experiments, their exact receipts, the existing primary-source literature notes, and the newly saved [paired diagnosis](/workspace/improvement/execution_tradeoff/saved_diagnosis.json). No GPU work, model downloads, held-out evaluation, interface changes, or frozen-file edits were performed for this note.

## Findings that determine the next experiment

The strongest inexpensive starting point is the saved **single-source 50-step** trajectory: source WER **83.9034%**, DNSMOS **3.1056**, NISQA **3.9766**, UTMOS **3.1981**, across all 48 recordings and 994 reference words. It fails the unchanged UTMOS ≥3.5 floor. Its measured optimization loop averages **19.6064 seconds/recording**, **2.6141 seconds/second of audio**, with median **18.8228** and p95 **27.6107 seconds**. This is an observed operating point, not a qualifying method or a practical-latency requirement. [Exact current diagnosis](/workspace/improvement/execution_tradeoff/saved_diagnosis.json); [frozen source scores](/workspace/improvement/execution_scaled/fvpilot/source-summary.json).

The 50-step source errors include **520 substitutions, 82 deletions and 232 insertions**. Insertion inflation contributes substantially, but does not explain the entire result: substitutions plus deletions alone account for 60.5634 reference-error points. No machine-error decomposition establishes human intelligibility. The clean/attack spectral differences provide a concrete, cheap intervention to test before another long optimizer run.

The source-loss proxy demonstrably becomes less useful with more compute. In the BigVGAN branch, going from 200 to 500 steps raises mean weighted teacher-forced NLL **166.1005 → 244.4776**, while WER falls **80.1811 → 78.7726%**, NISQA falls **3.3091 → 2.7068**, and UTMOS falls **2.2951 → 1.6528**. The extra 300 steps worsen both quality and total computation without improving source WER. These are paired saved checkpoints, not independent runs. [Mel score receipts](/workspace/improvement/execution_scaled/melpilot/source-summary.json); [200-step trajectories](/workspace/improvement/execution_scaled/melpilot/traces/mel_eot_200); [500-step trajectories](/workspace/improvement/execution_scaled/melpilot/traces/mel_eot_500).

The retained-decoder-graph alternative should first be compared on short/median/long exposed clips, then on all **48** performance points. Keep the exact source models, precision, objective, optimizer, initialization, update count, projection and output contract. Check gradient/trajectory agreement and final WAV equality where deterministic execution permits it; otherwise predeclare numeric tolerances and disclose differences. Retaining decoder activations while source activations exist may exceed 24 GB, so memory is an outcome. The old sequential detached-source implementation deliberately traded memory for recomputation.

## Why the existing constraints do not establish content preservation

The frozen perceptual terms are **absolute** temporal variation `TV(z)/TV(q)` and high-frequency energy fraction `HF(y)/HF(x)`. They are not distances between original and attacked speech. [Loss definitions](/workspace/beyondwaveform/losses.py:8); [clean denominators](/workspace/beyondwaveform/losses.py:49); [explicit-reflection variant](/workspace/scripts/probe_deterministic_execution.py:39). A lower high-frequency fraction can satisfy that term while removing speech detail. A temporally smooth representation need not contain the original phonemes. This is a mathematical limitation of the objective, not proof that either term caused every audible failure.

The saved means are consistent with that limitation: FV ensemble-EOT HF ratio falls **0.9702 → 0.4077**, while final UTMOS is only **3.0850**. BigVGAN's ratio falls **0.5294 → 0.2708**, while final UTMOS is **1.6528**. FV final TV remains around **1.023**, whereas BigVGAN rises to **1.410**. NLL growth dominates the finite regularizers; the lexical NLL has no explicit ceiling or decoded-word-success saturation. The first trace row evaluates the zero-edit loss but records radius after the first update; the last trace loss evaluates the iterate immediately before its saved final update. These distinctions prevent interpreting the means as exact saved-WAV objective values. [Optimizer receipt fields](/workspace/improvement/scaled_attack.py:51).

An off-distribution feature or mel perturbation can therefore preserve a global latent norm yet change spectral envelopes or phonetic detail after nonlinear synthesis. Good zero-delta reconstruction only qualifies the decoder near its natural inputs. It does not qualify optimized inputs. The BigVGAN reconstruction/attack contrast already demonstrates this distinction locally; it does not establish that all vocoders or generators fail. [Reconstruction and attack results](/workspace/improvement/15_scaled_results.md).

## Ranked falsifiable next hypotheses

**1. Partial reference-magnitude anchoring can repair some attack-added spectral damage at negligible incremental synthesis cost.** Start from the saved single50 attack, retain its STFT phase, and move its log magnitudes a fixed modest fraction toward a declared reference before one inverse STFT. A zero-delta FV reconstruction reference tests restoration toward the same synthesis path; original-clean magnitudes test restoration toward the original recording. They are different interventions: choose and freeze the primary reference/strength before scoring. Do not silently select an alpha per recording using quality or WER.

The new diagnosis shows mean relative magnitude error **0.6337** for single50 versus **0.3302** for reconstruction; frame-envelope error is **0.2764** versus **0.1333**. These observations support an attack-added distortion hypothesis. The weak single50 UTMOS/log-magnitude rank correlation (**−0.2842**) prevents claiming that magnitude damage is the dominant cause. [Paired diagnosis](/workspace/improvement/execution_tradeoff/saved_diagnosis.json).

Use unprocessed single50, the STFT→inverse-STFT identity round trip, and the reconstruction as controls. Keep duration, amplitude handling and window/hop explicit, and measure the achieved waveform spectrum after inverse STFT. An arbitrary edited complex spectrogram need not be exactly realizable: inverse STFT returns a least-squares waveform under its overlap/window conditions. The [official inverse-STFT documentation](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.14/generated/torch.istft.html) supports that mathematical point; installed execution remains PyTorch **2.4.1+cu121**, so verify the local operator rather than assuming a library upgrade. A single projection is sufficient for this diagnostic; iterative phase reconstruction introduces a different cost and signal change.

**Main risk:** Whisper itself consumes magnitude-derived log-mel features. Restoring magnitudes may remove the adversarial evidence directly, even while retaining attacked phase. The available WER headroom is only **8.9034 points**, while UTMOS needs to rise **0.3019**. A joint pass is uncertain. Spectral-loss precedent exists in [Parallel WaveGAN](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.11480) and its [spectral-convergence/log-magnitude implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kan-bayashi/ParallelWaveGAN/master/parallel_wavegan/losses/stft_loss.py); those training results do not validate postprocessing adversarial recordings or imply novelty here.

**Accept only** full-cohort WER >75%, DNSMOS ≥3, NISQA ≥3.75 and UTMOS ≥3.5, with measured added generation cost. A reasonable *proposed*, non-user-mandated engineering target is restoration taking ≤5% of the existing single50 optimization time: the restoration is justified as cheap only if its measured overhead is small beside the retained teacher search. Reject the repair if quality improves by erasing the needed WER, or if the round-trip control explains the change. Transfer/defense retention remains unmeasured until the fixed candidate qualifies.

**2. One retained decoder graph can reduce latency while preserving the same quality/attack operating point.** Benchmark the exact implementation change above separately from magnitude anchoring. Pass requires lower measured generation wall time and seconds/audio-second without changing the matched attack/quality outputs and without exceeding available memory. A runtime gain alone does not solve UTMOS; combining it with hypothesis 1 requires separate controls so each effect is attributable. Set the numerical speed target after the component profile reveals how much duplicate decoder time exists; total speedup cannot exceed removal of that measured fraction without other changes.

**3. Saturating already-large token loss can avoid spending extra runtime damaging speech after useful recognition error has saturated.** This is a later objective experiment if cheap restoration fails. Use a declared per-token ceiling or margin objective, preserve the same 48 recordings and budgets, and compare with original NLL at equal wall time and source-gradient calls. Prediction: less late-stage distortion and earlier useful endpoints; rejection: lower WER at every quality-admissible point. Existing decoding-aware R1 failures and [MORE's staged accuracy/length objectives](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01852v2) limit novelty claims. This is an inference from the local NLL/WER divergence, not an already validated remedy.

## Costs and evidence boundaries

For **single50 only**, measured optimization throughput implies approximately **15.69 minutes for 48 similar pilot recordings**, or **5.45 hours for 1,000 similarly distributed recordings**, before loading, teacher-data preprocessing, output I/O and evaluation. Duration-based extrapolation gives **26.14 allocated GPU-hours for ten audio hours**, assuming throughput carries over. Whisper's fixed context and decoder length dependence mean this extrapolation must be presented as conditional; the actual longer training-clip distribution needs its own benchmark. These forecasts must not use the 45h51m four-trajectory queue as the cost of one method.

Saved audio can make the *incremental research test* cheap; new-audio deployment still pays the full teacher optimization plus any restoration. A learned student might change that, but no new successful student exists. Report teacher-target generation, preprocessing, training and inference separately, then calculate break-even using **total teacher + training cost divided by per-new-input time saved**. Teacher and student must independently retain the WER/quality gate and later transfer/defense behavior. More data or a one-forward architecture does not demonstrate that result.

This audit does not establish intelligibility, unseen-model transfer, preprocessing persistence, current GPU availability, exact component timing, or memory of the proposed implementation. Preserve the frozen negative selection and untouched evaluation sets. The next 48-point pilot must retain full denominators, detached execution, logs/heartbeats, explicit exit receipts and a declared time ceiling.
