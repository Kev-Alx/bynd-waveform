# Three methodological departures and their falsifiable hypotheses

Prepared 21 October 2026 (Asia/Taipei). **Planning only: steps 1–4. No method was implemented, trained, or newly benchmarked.** Historical measurements are documented in `01_original_method_and_baseline.md` and `baseline_evidence.json`; source checks are in `03_literature_review.md`. See `data_and_evaluation_constraints.md` for split and threat-model constraints.

## The scientific decision

Can a different method increase **zero-target-query transfer failure while preserving the original human-readable utterance**, at a declared quality and resource budget? Whisper-small surrogate WER alone cannot answer this. The current bottleneck is the attack–quality frontier: the historical 999-clip stronger-budget run has 74.11% WER / 2.792 UTMOS; the paper-budget run has 64.74% / 3.358. The repeatedly used 24-clip strong control has 74.74% / 3.319, while its quality control has 45.68% / 3.676. These are development/history observations, not new results.

Loss reductions, learning rates, HF weights, normalization, smoothing, averaging, unmatched feature layers, and Adam coordinates have already been explored. The recent channel_std probe gains 0.080 UTMOS while losing 0.63 WER points on the same 24 clips, still below the paper quality anchor. Retain it as a sensitivity control. Removing perceptual terms also failed to close the quality gap. These small changes should not consume the next research budget.

| Rank | Direction | Fundamental departure | Scientific value / subjective chance of useful signal | Cost / implementation difficulty | Principal risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | R1: decoded sequence-risk objective | Structured sequence prediction replaces teacher-forced token NLL | High value / medium chance: directly addresses source-loss mismatch, transfer uncertain | 2–4× FV scoring; moderate implementation | Source-specific or insertion-only gains; discrete candidate collapse |
| 2 | R2: content-anchored local-neighbor search | A low-dimensional local mixture space replaces the arbitrary feature ball | High value / medium-low chance: mechanism clear, feasible directions may be too restrictive | 1–2× FV plus retrieval; moderate implementation | Mixtures may still leave speech support or remove all useful directions |
| 3 | R3: learned waveform residual policy | Waveform temporal network and amortized learning replace per-example FV search | High value / medium-low chance: repeatable directions plausible, cross-architecture transfer uncertain | Up to 24 hours primary training; high implementation | Poor transfer; training may never amortize |
| Conditional | R4: matching codec/diffusion resynthesis | Replace encoder–vocoder representation entirely | Medium value / low initial feasibility confidence until reconstruction audit | Potentially 1–6 hours/24 clips; high integration | Close prior art, reconstruction contract, old dependencies, 24 GB fit |

Chance assessments are subjective planning judgments, not empirical probabilities. Ranks express prospective scientific value, not predicted performance. Each direction gets an independent mechanism test before combinations are considered. Full experiments are specified in `04_experimental_plan.md` and `experiment_matrix.csv`.

## R1 — Optimize decoded sequence risk

**H1.** If token NLL mostly exploits source-specific probability changes, a source-only N-best expected edit-risk objective will improve cross-architecture transfer at matched content, quality, and generation compute relative to the same FV optimizer. Reject if source risk increases without final transfer gain, if improvement vanishes at matched quality, or if human intelligibility deteriorates. The 48-clip confirmation stage uses the public surrogate only; held-out transfer is evaluated at the final stage.

Retain the compatible frozen WavLM layer-6 / feature-conditioned HiFi-GAN pair, rho=0.1, and clean-referenced TV/HF terms. Every five updates, decode K=4 hypotheses using **Whisper-small only**, detach hypotheses, and score their lexical sequence probabilities. Include the correct transcript. With H the candidate set, maximize

`R(x) = sum_h softmax(s_s(h|x)/tau) * min(edit(h,y)/max(1,|y|),1)`.

Here `s_s` is the log probability of the lexical sequence **plus its terminal EOS**, divided by lexical-token length plus one, and tau=1. Prompt/language/task tokens are excluded. Edit distances and candidate construction are detached. EOS stops candidate construction. An empty decoded lexical sequence is retained with edit cost one, score equal to source log p(EOS | prompt,x), and normalization length one; it does not receive an artificial zero lexical score. If source output becomes empty on more than 5% of development utterances, stop the condition as degenerate rather than claiming a sequence-risk gain. An EOS-excluded nonempty-candidate variant is a separate ablation to test whether any gain comes from termination pressure rather than word confusions. This is a surrogate for **word** edit risk despite token-scored paths. The bounded edit cost prevents arbitrarily long hypotheses from dominating optimization; standard unbounded WER remains an evaluation metric. Decoder refresh and hypothesis scoring count toward compute. A flat risk gradient or no incorrect candidate is a recorded failure; one predeclared fallback may add source-generated edit candidates, reported as a separate condition.

Bounded risk and sum NLL have different units: freeze a single scale c from median physical-feature gradient-norm ratios on three declared duration-stratified development clips, then optimize `-cR + original_perceptual_loss`. Require finite nonzero risk gradients; otherwise declare degeneracy. This calibration and an uncalibrated c=1 control are specified in the experimental plan. First change the loss only. Measure NLL versus decoded risk, gradient cosine with NLL, source substitutions/deletions/insertions, N-best diversity and weight entropy, and quality-matched feature displacement. An optional phone-margin condition uses a separate frozen aligner and is explicitly an **additional-surrogate/data track**; none of the final CTC evaluation targets can serve as that aligner. A frozen phonetic representation is a diagnostic, not proof of preserved human semantics.

**Prior-art boundary.** [Minimum WER Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.01818) studies minimizing expected risk for ASR training; it does not establish that adversarial maximization transfers. [MORE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01852) already uses hypothesis-aware attack objectives for inference efficiency and EOS/repetition effects. Therefore “decoding-aware attack” is not new by itself. Any distinct contribution would need evidence for source-only sequence risk improving quality-constrained cross-architecture transfer, without latency or muting shortcuts.

## R2 — Search local feature geometry under content constraints

**H2.** If unconstrained perturbed features leave the vocoder's supported region, restricting search to small local clean-feature mixtures will improve quality at matched attack effect, or transfer at matched quality. Reject if the frontier does not move, simple norm shrinking reproduces the effect, or human content changes.

The initial **same-data** condition uses the current utterance's clean WavLM features as a bank. Each frame has at most four temporal neighbors plus itself. Select neighbors from a ±2-frame window with clean-feature cosine similarity at least 0.95; include q_t as an exact reconstruction option. Optimize

`z_t = (1-a_t) q_t + a_t sum_j w_tj q_j`,

with `a_t in [0,1]`, simplex weights w, the same global rho=0.1 constraint, and the hard content-anchor constraint `cos(z_t,q_t) >= 0.95` for every nonzero frame. Original TV supplies temporal regularization; there is no unspecified new weight-continuity penalty. Frames without valid neighbors remain q_t. Only mixture logits and a_t are optimized. Projection must preserve the convex set and radius together; convex interpolation back toward q does so. No claim is made that convex mixtures are guaranteed to belong to a true speech manifold.

The **added-data** extension builds a donor bank from allowed train-clean-100 speakers after excluding every development/confirmation/final speaker and duplicate. It uses no final-target output for retrieval. Freeze bank size, acoustic neighbor rule, clean speaker/content anchors, and retrieval cost. A bank with different speakers can introduce speaker drift; report it, rather than assuming kNN replacement is content-preserving. Matching encoder/vocoder training contracts remain essential: arbitrary feature layers or normalization are not interchangeable with the available layer-6-trained vocoder.

Keep source NLL fixed initially. Compare a same-rank random local basis, unrestricted delta rescaled to the achieved norm, and no-attack mixtures. These distinguish geometry from parameter count, effective radius, or resynthesis damage. Clean self-similarity, F0/voicing, duration, and feature anchors are development diagnostics; human transcription determines semantics. Since every generated waveform can algebraically be written x+residual, generation through a vocoder does not itself prove a distinct natural-speech manifold. A reconstruction-centered waveform attack is an essential causal control.

**Prior-art boundary.** [kNN-VC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18975) establishes nearest-neighbor SSL voice conversion. [SMACK](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity23/presentation/yu-zhiyuan-smack), [GATAS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09833), and [DAA](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v260/wang25f.html) already explore semantic/prosodic, phoneme-level, or diffusion-latent ASR adversarial generation. The proposed distinction is zero-target-query local SSL search versus an unrestricted FV ball at a common content/quality/resource budget. Novelty remains unresolved.

## R3 — Learn a waveform residual policy across utterances

**H3.** If transferable attack directions recur across speakers, a small input-conditioned waveform network can learn them without a vocoder and reduce per-utterance generation cost at comparable transfer, content, and quality. Reject the attack claim if improvement requires weaker constraints; reject the efficiency claim if lifetime training plus generation does not break even at the declared deployment volume.

Use a 1-D temporal convolutional U-Net with at most 3 million trainable parameters, 16 kHz mono input, and a same-length residual output. Freeze exact widths/strides before training; use deterministic padding/cropping with no duration change, prefix insertion, muting, or transcript editing. Bound residual amplitude and RMS with the same registered ladder as waveform PGD. Initially train with frozen Whisper-small sum-NLL maximization, a specified multi-resolution log-STFT distance, and normalized frozen-WavLM feature distance. The plan gives exact formulas and one-time source-gradient-calibrated weights. This spectral distance is not a psychoacoustic masking model; a Qin-style masking condition remains a separate baseline. The content encoder adds pretrained resources and cost even though no vocoder is used. This isolates architecture and amortization; do not combine R1 until independently supported. A five-step source-only refinement is a second condition, with its cost counted.

Training needs **newly acquired** train-clean-100; it is not currently local. Use nested, speaker-balanced **1-hour and 10-hour** training subsets chosen deterministically by duration before any outcomes, whole utterances or transcript-aligned crops, batch one and gradient accumulation. The 1-hour subset is the initial training condition; the 10-hour subset is optional scaling. Report the resulting utterance counts rather than assuming 2,000 utterances equals a fixed duration. Exclude all development/confirmation/final speakers and audio/text duplicates. Disclose unavoidable pretrained-model exposure separately. Use three independent training seeds, each capped at 12,000 updates or eight measured GPU-hours, whichever comes first. Select checkpoints using source-only risk and quality on training-disjoint development speakers. Sequential content/source gradient computation or checkpointing may be needed for the one 24 GB GPU.

Compare an input-independent universal residual, a comparable-parameter FV residual head trained on the **same data and objective**, and the waveform head with five-step refinement. The FV-head comparison tests vocoder necessity; it is an architecture ablation, not the primary method. Compare the nested 1-hour and 10-hour subsets only at matched optimizer-update/compute budgets, then separately show the data-plus-compute scaling curve. Universal/FV-head/data ablations are conditional finalists drawing from the separate data/ablation allocation, not all mandatory three-seed retrainings within the R3 28-hour cap. An inference-only speedup cannot hide training costs.

**Prior-art boundary.** [AudioShield / Whispering Under the Eaves](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.00858) already learns universal transferable VITS-latent perturbations; [Watch What You Pretrain For](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.13523) studies SSL-related ASR attack transfer. Neither universal transfer nor latent learning is novel. This experiment tests an input-conditioned waveform policy against per-example waveform/FV methods, controlling data, human content, and lifetime compute.

## Data and training alternatives are separate factors

- **Core, no added data/surrogate:** R1, per-utterance R2, paper controls, independent waveform PGD, and momentum attacks use the same current utterance/transcript and common frozen public components.
- **Added clean data:** R2 donor bank and R3 generator use audited [LibriSpeech train-clean-100](https://www.openslr.org/12) (CC BY 4.0). Learned comparisons receive identical manifests. Acquisition and training are future work.
- **Added surrogate / pseudo-label:** phone supervision or a second generation surrogate is a separate resource/threat track. It cannot be a held-out target, and pseudo-label uncertainty/filtering must be reported.
- **Domain shift:** reserve external English speech before selection. FLEURS is a candidate, not verified speaker-disjoint data; metadata must be inspected. MLS shares LibriVox origins and needs source-level duplicate checks. AISHELL-1 is later Mandarin generalization, with separate surrogate/vocoder and preprocessing contracts.
- **Source-only augmentation:** a fixed RIR/noise/resampling distribution can test EOT after mechanisms are isolated. Apply it to both control and candidate, include a non-EOT ablation, and count all gradient passes. No target feedback.
- **R4 optional representation:** a matched codec/diffusion pair must pass reconstruction and license/24 GB gates first. [Codec-Robust Attacks on Audio LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20519) already combines codec latents and codec-aware optimization; DAA already uses DiffVC. A codec swap alone is not a novelty claim.

## Bounded exploration and contribution threshold

The proposed 120 GPU-hour envelope is an illustrative maximum scenario, not an approved user budget or a job reservation. A lean 24-hour feasibility scenario is defined in the experimental plan. The full scenario reserves **56 hours (46.7%) for R1–R3**, before optional mechanism ablations. Each family gets one initial design, one mechanism-supported repair at most, and at most three predeclared development operating points. No open-ended coefficient/LR search. Stop a family if reconstruction/content fails, or no development Pareto signal remains after its allowance. Stop a model that cannot fit 24 GB after batch-one/checkpointing and one reduced-width attempt; label any reduced version honestly.

A contribution would require untouched final transfer data, speaker-cluster uncertainty, independent training-seed evidence, human transcription and quality measurements, relevant strong baselines, full data/compute accounting, and a verified distinction from close prior art. This planning package reports no such new performance evidence.
