# Scalable generative search: prior work and executable recommendations

Prepared 22 October 2026, Asia/Taipei. This is a primary-source research note, not a result report. No weights were downloaded and no GPU inference or training was run for this note. The current 48-point results remain negative; nothing below establishes >75% WER, transfer improvement, or defense resistance.

## Recommendation

Make **heterogeneous-surrogate, transformation-aware feature-vocoder optimization** the next main experiment. Preserve the existing WavLM/HiFi-GAN resynthesis path, which already gives a useful quality operating point; change the optimization to reward errors that persist across a newly declared public surrogate and registered input transformations. Allow longer optimization with explicit quality/content checkpoints. This attacks the two requested properties directly: cross-model transfer and persistence after preprocessing. Larger source models are useful only if they add complementary gradients; parameter count alone is not the mechanism.

Use **112M-parameter BigVGAN-v2 mel-space optimization** as the main independent decoder fallback. It imports a substantially more capable pretrained synthesis model and changes the search geometry, without needing attack-generator training. Require a reconstruction qualification before optimizing any attack. A failed reconstruction or a failed 48-recording attack/quality gate ends that configuration. DiffVC is a useful external baseline or later study, but not the best first replacement on one 24GB card: DAA already attacks its latent, its inference wrappers disable gradients, and full denoising differentiation adds memory and stochasticity.

The main path is more executable than a new diffusion pipeline; BigVGAN is a genuine methodological alternative to the existing compatible SSL decoder. Neither is guaranteed to beat 75%. A larger learned U-Net with the same failed R3 objective would not identify or fix the observed near-identity solution and should not be the next expensive run.

## What the completed local experiments actually say

The paired 48-recording feature baseline reaches 49.7988% source WER with UTMOS 3.5789; extending its trajectory to 100 steps reaches 65.4930% but lowers UTMOS to 3.3105. Direct global-budget waveform optimization can reach 58.7525%, but NISQA falls to 3.4367. The later clean-energy constraint reaches only 29.1751%; it also loses to the RMS-matched global control. These results argue against another tighter waveform constraint as the primary direction. They indicate an attack/quality tradeoff and an imperfect source-loss proxy, not proof that speech-preserving WER above75% is impossible. See [changes and goals](/workspace/improvement/11_changes_and_goals.md), [48-point mechanisms](/workspace/improvement/08_pilot48_results.md), [100-step results](/workspace/improvement/10_refinement_results.md), and [energy-envelope results](/workspace/improvement/13_envelope_results.md).

The paper's 75.43% is not an already matched joint benchmark: scorer settings, study population, and several author implementation choices are unrecovered. Earlier 24-recording feature ablations exceeded75% source WER but failed the declared UTMOS floor. A new75% source result therefore needs common-cohort controls and evidence of quality; it would still not establish75% held-out transfer. [Local baseline audit](/workspace/improvement/01_original_method_and_baseline.md).

## DiffVC and DAA: direct prior-art overlap

Wang et al.'s **Diffusion-based Adversarial Attack to Automatic Speech Recognition**, ACML2024 proceedings published in PMLR2025, uses DiffVC and adversarial latent perturbation for targeted ASR attacks. Its public abstract reports an average101-point WER increase over clean and25points over C&W; these are different experiments and cannot be imported as this project's expected improvement. [Official paper page](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v260/wang25f.html).

The paper places a bounded perturbation on the reverse-diffusion latent and discusses signed/skip-gradient optimization. Its description explicitly includes pitch, rhythm, and semantics among the editable attributes. Consequently a generic proposal to optimize a diffusion voice-conversion latent for ASR error is already covered, and high reference WER alone cannot distinguish successful adversarial preservation from changed spoken words. The paper's targeted setting also differs from this study's untargeted, preserved-original-content endpoint. [Official full paper, equations6–8 and surrounding discussion](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v260/main/assets/wang25f/wang25f.pdf).

I did not locate an author-owned DAA attack implementation/checkpoint release from the PMLR page or title/GitHub searches. That is a discovery limitation, not proof none exists. The underlying **DiffVC** implementation and conversion weights are public:

These links are recorded in the [official README](https://github.com/huawei-noah/Speech-Backbones/blob/main/DiffVC/README.md). Its frontend is80mel/22.05kHz/hop256, not the current WavLM1024-channel/16kHz contract. [Official parameters](https://github.com/huawei-noah/Speech-Backbones/blob/main/DiffVC/params.py).

Differentiability is not turnkey. `DiffVC.forward` and its encoder wrapper use `@torch.no_grad`; the reverse-diffusion and decoder forward wrappers also do so. The maximum-likelihood sampler injects random noise at reverse steps, whereas probability-flow sampling is deterministic conditional on the initial latent. A research adapter would need a gradient-enabled sampler, frozen weights, fixed/replayed noise, and finite-difference gradient checks. Removing only the outer decorator is insufficient. [Conversion implementation](https://github.com/huawei-noah/Speech-Backbones/blob/main/DiffVC/model/vc.py), [diffusion implementation](https://github.com/huawei-noah/Speech-Backbones/blob/main/DiffVC/model/diffusion.py).

For24GB, freezing weights does not remove saved intermediate activations for latent gradients. Full differentiation through K denoising steps scales the retained graph with K absent checkpointing; an approximate skip gradient changes the optimizer and needs an ablation against actual derivative checks. There is no inspected official24GB end-to-end attack-memory measurement. Any claim of fitting the longest24-second recording would be an unmeasured inference. Self-reference conversion should be used initially, since changing the reference speaker creates a separate voice-conversion confound.

## BigVGAN-v2: best available independent decoder test

NVIDIA releases a24kHz,100mel,256x,112M-parameter BigVGAN-v2 pretrained generator. The v2 model uses a large, diverse audio compilation; the model card identifies multilingual speech, environmental sounds and instruments. This imports training scale without training a new model locally. Both official code and the model card declare MIT. [NVIDIA model card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/bigvgan_v2_24khz_100band_256x), [code license](https://github.com/NVIDIA/BigVGAN/blob/main/LICENSE).

The generator is a convolutional decoder with periodic nonlinearities and anti-aliased activations; its reachable waveforms and mel conditioning differ from the current feature-conditioned HiFi-GAN. This makes it a plausible test of whether higher-quality resynthesis leaves more room for adversarial editing. It is a hypothesis, not evidence that a112M decoder automatically transfers better. BigVGAN's published contribution is universal high-quality vocoding, not ASR adversarial robustness. [Original paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.04658), [generator implementation](https://github.com/NVIDIA/BigVGAN/blob/main/bigvgan.py).

Acquisition can fetch only the approximately450MB generator and tiny config/license; the full3.8GB repository also contains unnecessary discriminator/optimizer and earlier checkpoint files. [Official file listing](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/bigvgan_v2_24khz_100band_256x/tree/main).

An implementation should hash downloaded bytes; the values above are source revisions, not an invented downloaded-file SHA256 receipt. Download caches, vendored code and all receipts should be explicitly directed inside `/workspace/improvement`.

**Critical gradient trap:** the fused CUDA activation's backward raises `NotImplementedError`. Set `use_cuda_kernel=False`; use the PyTorch activation path, leave autograd enabled for mel input, and set decoder parameters `requires_grad=False`. The model-card inference example's `torch.inference_mode()` is inappropriate for attack optimization. [Fused implementation](https://github.com/NVIDIA/BigVGAN/blob/main/alias_free_activation/cuda/activation1d.py), [PyTorch implementation](https://github.com/NVIDIA/BigVGAN/blob/main/alias_free_activation/torch/act.py).

Use the checkpoint's exact mel operator:24kHz,100bands, FFT/window1024, hop256, Slaney filterbank, Hann window, explicit reflection padding and logarithmic compression. The `fmax:null` configuration means Nyquist; it does not mean the source ASR's6kHz high-frequency cutoff. The waveform is resampled back to16kHz through a differentiable, fixed resampler for the source loss and saved evaluation; record a resampling-only control. Fixed padding/trimming must preserve native duration. [Official configuration](https://github.com/NVIDIA/BigVGAN/blob/main/configs/bigvgan_v2_24khz_100band_256x.json), [official mel extraction](https://github.com/NVIDIA/BigVGAN/blob/main/meldataset.py).

NVIDIA's reported low inference-memory benchmarks do not measure attack backpropagation through generator plus ASR plus content losses. Do not infer24GB fit from them. Before the48-point condition, benchmark the shortest/median/longest already exposed recordings, including an actual latent backward and all sequential loss passes. Preserve the same complete recordings across controls. Gradient checkpointing may reduce memory; waveform chunking without sufficient decoder context changes the forward function and is not automatically an exact fix. [NVIDIA benchmark](https://github.com/NVIDIA/BigVGAN/blob/main/README.md?plain=1).

Suggested frozen fallback algorithm, to preregister before inference:

1. Compute clean mel `m0`; reconstruct `v(m0)` and score all48 exposure recordings. Require the existing G0 reconstruction rules: WER no more than2points above clean and UTMOS within0.15 of the old matched reconstruction. Also inspect DNSMOS/NISQA, amplitude and duration. An attack search cannot repair a failed reconstruction baseline scientifically.
2. If G0 passes, optimize `m=m0+delta` through the frozen decoder using a registered mel-space bound, native-source NLL and clean waveform/content penalties. Start at zero. Do not transplant the WavLM Frobenius radius numerically into log-mel units and call the budgets equivalent. Freeze at most three declared operating points and a longest-run cap.
3. Use the same source/transform objective and controls as the main feature path where feasible; report both equal-update and equal-gradient/compute comparisons. Include BigVGAN reconstruction and a norm-matched random-mel condition. Save intermediate candidate WAVs only at registered steps; select one source-only quality-admissible trajectory point before any new held-out target evaluation.
4. If an admissible setting achieves at least a3-point source WER improvement over the best admissible existing feature control, or reaches75% source WER, freeze it and advance to the separate exposed76-recording validation. A decoder that cannot beat the quality-admissible feature Pareto frontier should stop; do not treat model size as success.

These are proposed criteria, not changes to an existing frozen execution plan. Formal content preservation remains a human endpoint. Maximizing transcript NLL while constraining WavLM distance can still remove or replace a phoneme, so the content term is a guard and not proof.

## Codec latent search: available, but a less decisive fallback

Descript Audio Codec provides native16kHz and24kHz weights and explicitly releases weights under MIT. Its encoder returns quantized continuous decoder inputs and pre-quantization projected latents; the decoder accepts a continuous tensor. This offers a third search space without a diffusion chain. [Author README](https://github.com/descriptinc/descript-audio-codec/blob/main/README.md?plain=1), [model implementation](https://github.com/descriptinc/descript-audio-codec/blob/main/dac/model/dac.py).

The official downloader maps16kHz/8kbps to release0.0.5 [weights_16khz.pth](https://github.com/descriptinc/descript-audio-codec/releases/download/0.0.5/weights_16khz.pth) and24kHz/8kbps to0.0.4 [weights_24khz.pth](https://github.com/descriptinc/descript-audio-codec/releases/download/0.0.4/weights_24khz.pth). Its default downloader writes outside the research artifact directory, so use an explicit local acquisition path. The inspected code revision is `c7cfc5d2647e26471dc394f95846a0830e7bec34`. [Official download map](https://github.com/descriptinc/descript-audio-codec/blob/main/dac/utils/__init__.py).

Optimizing the quantized continuous vector after the quantizer bypasses discreteness and may leave the training distribution; calling it a valid codec-token attack would be wrong. Optimizing before quantization produces zero/discontinuous true gradients, and a straight-through approximation must be disclosed and checked. Neither parameterization guarantees preservation through a later independent codec defense. Reconstruction quality and content controls are mandatory. Relative to BigVGAN, DAC's reconstruction task is appealing, but the codebook adds a second confound and no inspected evidence predicts a better ASR attack frontier.

AudioCraft is another source of codecs/diffusion decoders, but its main repository distinguishes MIT code from CC-BY-NC4.0 model weights. Do not infer a weight license from code licensing or interchange incompatible checkpoint formats. [AudioCraft license declaration](https://github.com/facebookresearch/audiocraft), [EnCodec integration notes](https://github.com/facebookresearch/audiocraft/blob/main/docs/ENCODEC.md?plain=1).

## Main path: improve transferable, preprocessing-persistent resynthesis

The existing frozen feature-vocoder transform is valuable because it already generates intelligible-looking speech at an admissible quality operating point. Keep that generative restriction and replace the single untransformed source objective with an expectation across a **declared heterogeneous public-source ensemble** and a small fixed transformation distribution. Calculate model/transform gradients sequentially and sum their declared weights, so the card need not retain every source graph at once. Preserve the raw untransformed branch to monitor source strength.

A suitable model expansion should include a CTC architecture that is **not one of the four sealed final checkpoints**. Those remain Whisper-base, Whisper-medium, `facebook/wav2vec2-large-960h-lv60-self`, and `facebook/hubert-large-ls960-ft`. A new checkpoint may share family/pretraining characteristics; disclose that and report resource-matched T2 controls. A model trained against during generation is a source forever for this study, regardless of whether its outputs are consulted only through gradients. [Frozen threat model and endpoint](/workspace/improvement/04_experimental_plan.md).

Normalize losses and gradient contributions explicitly. Whisper lexical sum-NLL and CTC loss have different lengths/scales; blindly summing them can make one model dominate. Freeze a token-normalized or calibrated weighting rule before outcome inspection, then publish raw component losses and gradient norms. Compare the identical longer single-source attack, ensemble without EOT, and ensemble with EOT on all48pilot utterances. Include model count, actual backward calls and elapsed time;100iterations over several sources/transforms is not a100-backward baseline.

Use transformations that test the requested persistence, such as registered resampling and mild low-pass filtering, through accurate differentiable paths. Optimize only declared surrogate transforms; evaluate withheld parameters/defenses after freezing the candidate. EOT is established adversarial methodology, not a novel contribution. Qin et al. already combine speech perceptual constraints and simulated environmental transformations for robust audio attacks. [ICML2019 primary source](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/qin19a.html). For nondifferentiable defenses, a claimed adaptive attack needs an explicitly validated backward approximation; absence of a useful gradient is not proof of defense resistance. [Athalye et al., ICML2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.00420).

Multi-source audio attacks are also prior work. Guo et al. propose random-gradient and dynamic-gradient-weighted ensembles for transfer to ASR APIs; simply adding an ensemble would not support a new-principle claim. [Original authors' paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.08811), [published paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s42400-023-00175-8). TransAudio uses contextualized word-level perturbations and score-matching regularization to reduce source overfitting; its content-editing endpoint is not directly interchangeable with the present untargeted preserved-speech endpoint. [TransAudio primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.15940). Recent inter-domain and gradient-aligned ensemble work also means adaptive weighting/alignment should be compared rather than claimed by name alone; those cited studies address audio classification or speaker recognition rather than this exact full-ASR problem. [IJCAI2025 AIE](https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2025/697), [Interspeech2024 gradient-aligned speaker ensemble](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2024/li24g_interspeech.pdf).

A possible paper contribution is **demonstrating which generative search geometry and cross-model/transformation objective preserve real linguistic content while improving held-out transfer at matched quality and resources**. That requires mechanism ablations and human evidence. Neither a model swap nor another NLL objective alone establishes it.

## Falsifiable stopping and graduation rules

These recommendations respect the current untouched confirmation48/final200 and do not silently convert exposed data into a final test.

| Stage | What should be measured | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering contract | Actual gradients, frozen weights, finite outputs, native16kHz/duration, model/hash bindings, longest-clip measured VRAM/time | Stop on contract failure; distinguish it from a mathematically valid negative experiment. |
| Main method, paired48 | Existing FV controls, longer single-source, ensemble-only, ensemble+EOT; all fixed quality means and failure accounting | Freeze one admissible candidate only if the registered source/quality mechanism improvement is useful; if source WER is near clean or quality collapses, do not run a long training job. |
| Decoder fallback G0 | BigVGAN reconstruction versus clean and existing reconstruction on48 | Stop if WER exceeds clean by2points or UTMOS drops by more than0.15. |
| Expanded exposed76 | Same frozen method/configuration and controls; no target-driven reselection | A failure narrows the hypothesis; do not relax floors or restart endless48-point searches. |
| Confirmation/final | Source-only one-use confirmation, then frozen held-out target and defense comparison plus human protocol | A75% source result is a milestone; held-out transfer/defense preservation requires its own paired measurements. |

The original four-target family-balanced transfer endpoint and3-point superiority criterion should remain visible. To substantiate the user's stronger goal, additionally preregister what **above75%** means: raw source WER, raw family-balanced held-out WER, or every target separately. Report all three descriptively rather than exploiting the ambiguity. For persistence, compare candidate and original feature control on identical attack WAVs, clean controls, defense implementations and decoder settings; report each defense separately and the worst degradation. The original matched-defense recipes that are unavailable remain unavailable—new LPF/EOT success cannot establish those missing claims.

Blinded original-content transcription is required before describing speech as preserved for people. Automatic MOS and SSL similarity can screen candidates, but cannot establish the registered human noninferiority criterion. Without human evidence, the honest paper endpoint is an automatic-quality, source/transfer robustness result with an explicit content limitation. A larger training set is useful only after a successful per-utterance teacher attack exists; then distill successful paired latent trajectories into an amortized policy and verify whether it retains quality and transfer, rather than asking the previous collapsed generator to learn from more hours of the same failing signal.
