## Evidence and hypothesis

The old waveform U-Net collapsed: its median edit used only0.0413% of the RMS budget, initialization was almost entirely DC, and all saved transcripts stayed clean. This does not establish that a shared model cannot attack speech. By contrast, per-recording feature edits already raise WER above75% but fail UTMOS, and cheap spectral repair traded away11.07WER points without reaching the quality floor. Duplicate-decoder removal saved14.63% but left a16.77-second per-recording search. Full beam5 feedback costs about4.30seconds/recording, so frequent decoded-feedback search could erase savings.

**Hypothesis:** a shared input-conditioned temporal policy on clean WavLM6 features can learn useful nonzero speech edits once, then generate on new recordings with one forward pass and one qualified HiFi-GAN decode. A violation-only quality correction may retain more natural speech than source-only training while avoiding identity collapse. It is trained directly through the frozen source, with no optimized teacher-target generation and no fresh per-recording optimization at deployment. A successful/fast inference result is unproven until measured.
